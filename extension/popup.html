Azerit — LinkedIn

This extension links your LinkedIn to Azerit. Nothing to set up here: open Azerit → Settings → LinkedIn and click Connect.

Invitations and messages are sent from your profile by Azerit, at a human pace — this browser doesn't need to stay open.
